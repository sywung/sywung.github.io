CloudSync

最後更新日期：2026-11-24

CloudSync 是個人開發者為自己使用而製作的 iPad／iPhone App，用來把 OneDrive 或 Google Drive 上的某個資料夾，與裝置上的某個資料夾（例如 Obsidian 的筆記庫）雙向同步。

隱私權政策

  • 只在您的裝置上執行。同步由 App 在 iPad／iPhone 上直接完成，資料只在您的裝置與您授權的雲端服務（Microsoft OneDrive、Google Drive）之間傳輸，不經過開發者或任何第三方的伺服器。
  • 只存取您選定的資料夾。App 只讀寫您在任務中選擇的雲端資料夾與裝置資料夾，用來比對差異、上傳、下載與刪除，以維持兩邊一致。
  • 不蒐集、不分享資料。App 不蒐集個人資料、不含廣告或分析工具，也不會把您的檔案、檔名或帳號資訊傳送、出售或分享給開發者或任何第三方。
  • 登入資訊只存在裝置上。登入後取得的存取權杖（token）以加密方式儲存在 App 的設定檔中，加密金鑰存在裝置的 Keychain。您可以隨時在 App 中登出，或在 Microsoft／Google 帳戶的安全性設定中撤銷授權。
  • 刪除資料。刪除 App 即會移除裝置上的設定與登入資訊；雲端與裝置上的檔案本身不會因刪除 App 而被刪除。

Google 使用者資料

CloudSync 使用 Google Drive API（https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive）列出、讀取、上傳與刪除您所選資料夾內的檔案，僅用於提供同步功能。CloudSync 對從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 規定。

CloudSync (English)

Last updated: 2026-11-24

CloudSync is an iPad/iPhone app made by an individual developer for personal use. It keeps a folder in OneDrive or Google Drive in two-way sync with a folder on the device (for example, an Obsidian vault).

Privacy Policy

  • Runs on your device only. Syncing is done by the app on your iPad/iPhone. Data travels only between your device and the cloud services you authorize (Microsoft OneDrive, Google Drive); it never passes through servers of the developer or any third party.
  • Accesses only the folders you choose. The app reads and writes only the cloud folder and device folder you pick in a sync task, to compare them and upload, download or delete files so both sides match.
  • No data collection or sharing. The app collects no personal data, contains no ads or analytics, and does not send, sell or share your files, file names or account information with the developer or anyone else.
  • Sign-in data stays on the device. Access tokens are stored encrypted in the app's configuration file, with the key kept in the device Keychain. You can sign out in the app at any time, or revoke access in your Microsoft or Google account security settings.
  • Deleting data. Deleting the app removes its settings and sign-in data from the device. Your files in the cloud and on the device are not deleted by removing the app.

Google user data

CloudSync uses the Google Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) to list, read, upload and delete files in the folder you select, solely to provide syncing. CloudSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
